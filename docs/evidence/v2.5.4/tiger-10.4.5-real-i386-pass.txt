Interpretation:
* Real iMac5,1 hardware picks the i386 slice. selected_arch=i386 in self-test
  confirms this from the compile-time __i386__ macro.
* Why i386 and not x86_64 despite EM64T-capable Core 2 Duo? Tiger 10.4.5 PREDATES
  the addition of x86_64 USERLAND support (added in 10.4.7). With no x86_64
  userland infrastructure, XNU's grade_binary scores x86_64=0 and falls through
  to i386. The bug-trigger path is unreachable on 10.4.5.
* Self-test passes 13/13. The i386 slice is unchanged in v2.5.4 from prior
  versions; v2.5.4 fixes don't touch the i386 build line (no relauncher needed,
  no weak frameworks, default 10.4 deployment).
* Confirms v2.5.4 is REGRESSION-FREE on real Tiger Intel hardware that doesn't
  trigger the bug-trigger code path. Real hardware vs. emulated CPU is a
  meaningful distinction here — proves the fix isn't an emulation artifact.
